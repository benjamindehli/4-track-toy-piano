A Kalikå Leksakspiano, a Swedish toy piano, was recorded to cassette tape and played back twice: at normal speed, and at half speed. Half speed drops the pitch an octave and stretches everything else with it, turning a small bright instrument into something slow and heavy.

The two sets can be layered on the same keys and mixed against one another, split across different parts of the keyboard, or layered and stretched further down for deeper sounds. Each speed layer has its own volume and its own tremolo rate and depth.

A damping switch decides whether notes ring on after the key is released, the way a toy piano does, or fade out quickly. A hi-fi switch turns the lo-fi processing on and off: switch it off and filtering, saturation and modulation are added, with the saturation growing as you raise the mixer levels.

Tape echo and room reverb impulse responses finish the chain. A Fulltone Tube Tape Echo recorded twice for stereo, and a Chase Bliss Audio & Meris CXM 1978 on a room setting.

72 stereo samples at 48 kHz / 24-bit, embedded in the plugin as lossless FLAC.

## Features

- Kalikå Leksakspiano recorded to cassette tape and played back at normal speed and half speed.
- Half-speed layer sounds an octave down, longer and softer.
- Three modes: Layered, Layered (Pitch Stretched) and Split.
- Independent volume, tremolo rate and tremolo depth for each speed layer.
- Damping switch: ring on after release, or fade out quickly.
- Velocity on or off.
- Hi-fi switch: turn it off for filtering, saturation and modulation that grow with the mixer level.
- Tape echo and room reverb impulse responses, each short or long.
- 72 stereo samples at 48 kHz / 24-bit, embedded losslessly (98 MB source library).
